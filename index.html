Quick Start

5-step setup — get running in minutes

  1. 1
    Add the frozen ice bottles first, then fill the cooler with water to the minimum fill line.
  2. 2
    Fully submerge the pump, then close the zipper to help maintain the water temperature.
  3. 3
    Place a thin dry cloth between the therapy pad and your skin. Secure the pad with the strap — snug, not tight.
  4. 4
    Plug in the machine, then select your preferred timer and flow setting.
  5. 5
    Place a towel or protective mat under the cooler to help keep surrounding surfaces dry.

BEFORE FIRST USE

Freeze six water bottles for at least 8 hours before use. For extended cooling, we recommend keeping a second set frozen and ready to swap.
Always add water before turning on the pump. Never run the pump dry, as this may damage the motor.
